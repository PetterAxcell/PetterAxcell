I am an AI research engineer with a background in computer science and telecommunications, and experience across research, software, infrastructure and entrepreneurship. My current work focuses on health information, language models and privacy.

## AI, healthcare and privacy

At Hospital Clínic de Barcelona, I work on AI applications for health information. My interests include clinical NLP, information structuring and explanation, language model assistants and evaluation.

Privacy is part of the engineering problem: how to prepare data, what information each component needs and how to assess the usefulness of a transformation. I have worked on text pseudonymisation and tabular anonymisation, alongside testing and documentation to make tool behaviour easier to review.

## Data people can explore and understand

For **AI and education**, I built an academic data storytelling project. It brings together evidence about teachers' adoption of AI, training, digital infrastructure and educational outcomes using sources including OECD and UNESCO.

The project connects data preparation, source documentation and an interactive interface. Educational indicators help explore differences between contexts; comparing them does not establish a causal relationship.

[Code, sources and methodology](https://github.com/PetterAxcell/pr2-equidad-educativa) · [Open visualisation](https://petteraxcell.github.io/pr2-equidad-educativa/)
